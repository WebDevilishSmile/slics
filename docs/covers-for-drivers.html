Get verified, once

Covers is for drivers on the on-call sheet, so an admin checks who you are before it opens.

  1. Scan the code and sign in. New to SLICs? Continue with Google sets you up, or tap Create one to use an email and password. It's free and there's nothing to download.
  2. You land on Covers. If you don't, open the menu (three lines, top left) and tap it. Then tap Verify I'm a driver.
  3. Type your Name the way the on-call sheet writes it, your UPS email (your employee ID @ups.com) and your Seniority date. Tap Send.
  4. An admin checks it against the on-call sheet. Once you're verified, Covers opens and a check mark shows next to your name.

Nothing is sent to your UPS email. Only admins see what you send, and your employee ID and seniority date are deleted once an admin decides.

What you'll see

  • Your place and call-in time for the week being bid, like "Cover 7, call in Thu at 9:15".
  • Every cover job you can bid on, with each day's start time and the route. Taken jobs are grayed out. Nobody sees who took a job.
  • Your list. Tap + on the jobs you want and put them in order. Taken ones drop off on their own. At your call-in time, tap Call dispatch and read it off.
  • Reminders, if you want them: before your call-in, when a job on your list is taken, and when the week's jobs are posted or change. Turn on Notifications in your profile.
  • Your week, on your profile: the job you're working or covering, with start times and route.

Good to know

  • Covers is for reference only. It's read from the on-call sheet and can be wrong or behind. Your list isn't a bid: you still pick by calling dispatch at your call-in time.
  • It's private. Other drivers never see your list, your place or your status.
  • Something doesn't match the sheet or dispatch? Tap Report a problem above the jobs. Not on the week's list and should be? Tap This is a mistake.
  • iPhone: reminders only work with SLICs on your home screen. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
