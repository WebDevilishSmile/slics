Directions, one step at a time

The directions PDFs are now steps you read in the app. Look up a SLIC, tap Directions and get the route, how to get in, and any maps of the yard. 65 SLICs have a route so far, and you can add the ones you know.

Read the directions

  1. Look up the SLIC and tap Directions on its card. The route opens as numbered steps, with how to get in at the end.
  2. More than one way? Tap the one you take at the top, like Way 2 or Way back. SLICs remembers it for next time.
  3. Tap Step by step for one big step at a time, with Back and Next buttons your thumb finds without looking. Read aloud says each step out loud.
  4. Tap Print to print the directions, or save them as a PDF.

Don't use your phone while driving. Read the steps before you pull out, or while you're stopped. While the directions are open, your screen stays on.

What else is in there

  • Getting in. Which gate, where to check in, where the empties go.
  • Maps & documents. Photos and PDFs such as a railyard map. Tap a photo to see it full screen.
  • Where the route came from. "From the original directions PDF", or "Driver-approved route" with the date it was approved.
  • Original PDF still opens the old sheet, for the SLICs that had one.

Open map or Directions?

  • Open map sends the address to Google Maps or Apple Maps, which route you like a car. Map routes may not be truck-friendly.
  • Directions is the route written down for that SLIC. Roads change, so if a step looks wrong or you're unsure, ask dispatch.
  • No Directions button? A SLIC may still show PDF instead, which opens the old sheet. No button at all means nobody has written the way yet.

Know a better way? Send it in.

Tap Suggest a change under the steps, or Know the way? Add directions under a SLIC that has none. Write one step per line. A line ending in a colon, like "Way back:", starts another way. Say why if you like, then tap Send to an admin. An admin checks it before drivers see it. Leave gate codes out: every driver would see them.
