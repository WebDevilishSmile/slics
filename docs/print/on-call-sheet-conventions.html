ON CALL SHEET: how the app reads the weekly cover tabs

What we've learned from the way dispatch keeps the sheet, and a few small asks · November 16, 2026

Dispatch runs the ON CALL SHEET and the bid. The SLICs app only follows along: it shows each cover driver the week's jobs, their place on the pick list and their call-in time, read from the sheet within a minute of each edit. The app never writes to the sheet, and can't. The sheet is the record and the app is a copy of it. The app reads the text in the cells and nothing else (no colors, no comments).

Where this comes from: the 30 weekly tabs from May to November 2026, and every change the app recorded from October 14 to November 11 (5,614 changed cells). Most of what follows is simply how the sheet is already kept, written down so the app can keep up with it. Where we ask for something, it's because the app stumbled on it at least once, and we say what happened. Dispatch has the final say on all of it.

A posted week, as dispatch keeps it today

  1. The tab's name is the week-ending Saturday's date: 11/21/2026
  2. The title row says the same date: JOBS AVAILABLE FOR W/E 11.21.2026
  3. The two header rows are as they've always been (Job # … Description, and NAME … PICK #7).
  4. Every job row has its Job #, listed once.
  5. Start times are 24-hour HH:MM, with a blank on days the job doesn't run.
  6. NAME on a job row is blank while the job is open, then holds the driver's name or CUT. A note starts with an asterisk: *COVER DAY-BY-DAY
  7. The pick list has every driver once, LAST, FIRST, spelled as the week before.
  8. Pick numbers run 1, 2, 3… down the list. 1 to 20 are the cover drivers; everyone after 20 is on call.
  9. A cover driver who's out keeps their row, with no call-in time and no number.
  10. The cell before PICK #1 says what the driver is doing that week (page 3).

What we've noticed when a week changes

  • Drivers see each edit within a minute. Changes made in one sitting reach them as one notification; spread over an afternoon, they become several.
  • When a driver picks, dispatch writes the job code on their row and their name on the job's row. On bid day the app watches for that row to fill to tell the next driver they're up, so it works best when it's typed during the call.
  • Renumbering the pick list: each driver hears their final place and time once, 30 minutes after the list settles.
  • Sorting or pasting whole rows keeps every column together. A single column moved on its own briefly puts drivers on the wrong jobs.
  • Bid day moved for a holiday? The sheet doesn't show the bid day, so a quick word to a SLICs admin is all the app needs. Thanksgiving (Thu 11/26) is W/E 12/05's bid day as things stand.
  • Something looks off in the app? The sheet is the record. If the sheet is right and the app isn't, that's ours to fix: please let a SLICs admin know.

App needs Without it, the app can't read the week or shows drivers something wrong.
Helps The app copes either way. A small ask that saves drivers or dispatch a step.

1 · The tab and the job list

The tab

  • App needsThe tab's name is the date only: 11/21/2026, the Saturday the week ends. The app reads only tabs named that way, which is handy: ON CALL 11/14/2026, HUB Drivers…, SHIFTERS and Copy of… are left alone.
  • App needsThe title row carries the same date. When a new week starts as a copy of the last one, the title is the easy thing to miss, and the app then saves nothing for that week.
    Seen: 2 tabs since May had no title row (5/16, 8/15) and 2 said another date (7/4 said 6.27, 8/22 said 8.20).
  • App needsThe two header rows stay as they are. The app finds each column by its header text, so rows and columns can be added anywhere; only a renamed header gets lost.
    Seen: identical in all 30 tabs.
  • App needsOne tab per week. With two tabs of the same date, the app reads the first one only.
  • HelpsAn idea, if it suits dispatch: build next week under a working name (DRAFT 11/28) and rename it to the date when it's ready. The app ignores a draft, so drivers get one "Cover jobs posted" for the finished week instead of following it as it's typed.
    Seen: W/E 11/21 was built in the open over four days: 166 jobs added and 142 removed along the way, for a list of 51.
  • App needsA hidden row is hidden from drivers in the app too (admins still see it, marked "Hidden in sheet"). That's how the shifter jobs at the top stay out of drivers' view today; unhidden, they'd show as jobs to bid on. The app goes only by Hide row: a row filtered out or squeezed small still shows. Sleeper jobs (ER and a number) are never shown to drivers either way.

Job #

  • App needsEvery job row has one. A row with times but no number shows drivers a job with no name.
  • App needsOne number, one job, for the whole week. Drivers' lists, the admins' "hide" and the change history all follow the number, so a number reused for a different job carries the old job's lists with it.
  • HelpsCapitals, no spaces: CPU2 rather than cpu2 or ER18013?. The app treats them as the same job; drivers just see it as typed.
  • HelpsA number on two rows (a two-driver sleeper team, a shifter job shown twice) works fine; hiding one hides both.
    Seen: 26 repeated numbers in 16 weeks, all ER or SH, plus CPU4 twice on 11/21.

NAME (who took the job)

  • App needsBlank means open. Any text in this cell and drivers see the job as taken. Notes for drivers about an open job show in Description.
  • App needsCUT shows the job as taken, which is right.
  • App needsAn asterisk marks a note: *COVER DAY-BY-DAY. It tells the app the words aren't a driver's name. Drivers see the job as taken, never the note; admins see it labeled "Note".
    Seen: about a dozen notes under NAME in 19 weeks (covered day by day, nobody to cover). Without the asterisk, the app took each for a driver by that name.
  • HelpsThe full name, as the pick list has it (DOE, JANE), lets the app tell which driver took the job. A last name alone marks it taken just the same, but the driver who took it is also told "a job on your list was taken".
    Seen: of 553 names since August, 61% were a last name only, 30% LAST, FIRST, 6% CUT. Each week 4 to 11 can't be matched to a pick-list name as written.

Sun to Sat (start times)

  • App needs24-hour HH:MM (07:00, 21:45), blank on a day the job doesn't run, with any words in Description.
    Seen: all 2,456 day cells since August are valid times. The only slips on record were half-typed ones (15:0, 8:).

Description (the route)

  • HelpsMost routes are already written as a day label, a colon, then stops joined by >:
    Mon-Fri: BETPA>LANPA>BETPA
    Sun: BETPA>NEWPA>BETPA   Mon, Thu: BETPA>AILPA
    Written this way, the app shows each day's route on the driver's week, and every stop opens that building's address, map and phone.
    Seen: 447 of 492 descriptions since August. The rest show as plain text.

Assigned Driver and Cover Reason

  • Drivers never see these two columns in the app. Admins do.
  • HelpsAssigned Driver spelled as on the Jobs tab lets admins match the two.
  • HelpsCover Reason reads best from a short list, spelled the same each week, for example: VACATION FMLA VACANT PEAK JOB NEW JOB EXTRA JOB COMBO WEEK COVERING ER18006 VOLUNTARY LAYOFF REDUCTION OF WORKFORCE P/H OTHER
    Seen: nearly 50 wordings in 16 weeks for about a dozen reasons (PEAK, PEAK JOB, PEAK ADD; VACANT, OPEN, OPEN JOB; VOLUTNARY LAYOFF).
  • HelpsPersonal details: anyone with the sheet's link can read it, so for a family or medical reason OTHER says enough.
  • HelpsA dropdown could save typing: Data → Data validation → Dropdown, with "Show a warning" so anything can still be typed. A one-time setup, if the sheet's owner wants it.

2 · The pick list

NAME

  • App needsLAST, FIRST, spelled the same every week. Each driver's SLICs account is linked to their name as the sheet spells it. Capitals, periods and spacing don't matter; a different word does (a nickname, an added middle initial), and the app then tells that driver they're not on the week's list.
    Seen: 1,100 rows since August, 122 drivers, not one spelled two ways. This is the part of the sheet that's kept best.
  • App needsEach driver once. Listed twice, a driver keeps the first row's place and time.
    Seen: one driver listed twice in 4 of the weeks since May.
  • App needsAn asterisk marks a row that isn't a driver: *VACANT. The app skips it; without the asterisk it's read as a driver with no number.
    Seen: a VACANT row on 8/29, read as a driver.

Call-in time (the first column)

  • App needs24-hour HH:MM, later as the numbers go up. It's the time the app shows the driver, with a reminder before it on bid day.
  • App needsBlank for a driver who's out. No time, no reminder. A filled job / status cell also stops the reminder: the app takes it the driver has picked or won't be calling.

Pick number (the column after NAME)

  • App needs1, 2, 3… in order, each number once. The app goes by these numbers alone: 1 to 20 are the week's cover drivers, everyone after 20 is on call. A repeated number puts a driver in the wrong place.
    Seen: a number repeated in 7 of the 15 weeks read since August. None in the last seven weeks.
  • App needsA cover driver who's out keeps their row with no number, and their number is skipped (4, blank, 6), so the drivers after them don't move.
  • HelpsNumbers only in this column. A note here (TWI AND NIGHT, PASS) leaves the row unnumbered. The job / status cell or a column right of PICK #7 holds notes without that.
    Seen: 55 rows since August, all after number 20, where it does no harm.
  • HelpsColors aren't read. Yellow fill, red names and gray rows mean nothing to the app; only what's typed in a cell does.

PICK #1 to PICK #7

  • These columns are mostly empty these days, and the app doesn't depend on them.

Job / status (the cell before PICK #1)

  • App needsWhat the driver is doing that week. The app shows each driver their own cell, as typed, at the top of their profile, and it's how the app knows who covered which job.
  • App needsA pick shows in two places: the driver's name in the job's NAME cell (drivers see the job as taken) and the job code here (the driver sees their week, with times and route).
    Seen: most weeks, 1 to 4 taken jobs have no driver's row pointing at them, and now and then a row names a job whose NAME is still blank, so the app still offers it.
  • App needsNew: "You're up" on bid day. About a minute after this cell goes from blank to filled, the app tells the next driver who hasn't picked that it's their turn (never more than an hour before their call-in). It works off this cell alone, so when it's filled during the call, the next driver hears right away.
  • App needsFilled before bid day, the app takes it the driver won't be calling (a leave, their own job): no reminder, and "You're up" passes them by. So we'd ask that ON CALL not go in ahead for drivers who will still call.
  • HelpsNo answer? A word here (PASS, NO ANSWER) lets the driver after them be told they're up.
  • HelpsThe same few words each week help the app and the drivers. What we see most:

  • HelpsShort and neutral reads best. The driver sees this cell on their phone, and the sheet is open to anyone with the link. A return date is fine (FMLA - RTW 11/30); medical detail isn't needed.
  • HelpsA dropdown could work here too (Data → Data validation → Dropdown, "Show a warning"), since a job code still has to be typed.

3 · How a change travels, and who does what

Within a minuteThe app re-reads the tab that was edited. Drivers opening Covers see the new cell.
At onceA driver whose list held a job that was just taken or removed is told which, and what's next on their list.
After 10 quiet minutesDrivers on that week's list are told once what changed: jobs added, removed, new times.
After 30 quiet minutesEach driver whose place or call-in time ended up different is told the final one.

On bid day, about a minute after a driver's job / status is filled, the next driver who hasn't picked is told they're up. "Quiet" means no more edits to that part of the sheet, so a change made in one sitting is one notification, while a change every twenty minutes is one each time. Edits to a week after its Saturday aren't read. A full re-read runs early every morning in case anything was missed. Notifications reach the drivers who turned them on.

  • A week is built Sunday to Wednesday, 13 to 10 days before it ends (72% of all job-list edits), in sessions around noon, 3 PM and early evening. Bid day, Thursday, is the names going in from 7 AM.
  • A new job goes in one cell at a time: number, reason, five start times, description. That's 8 saves, and until this month each one was a notification on the admins' phones. On 11/10 that was 88 in a day. They're digests now.
  • Inserting a row used to make every job under it drop out of the app until the new row's first cell was typed (17 jobs gone and back on 11/9). That was ours to fix, and it's fixed: rows can go in wherever they belong.
  • Renumbering cascades: one driver's number changes and everyone after moves. Three quarters of all pick-list edits are times and numbers.

SLICs staff (admins and dispatch in the app)

  • The sheet is dispatch's. We never edit it to fix the app. If the sheet is right and the app isn't, that's a bug for the SLICs admin.
  • Admin → Covers is the hub. Cover Jobs has Refresh, the list of changes and the hide button. Cover Reports is where drivers' "Report a problem" lands.
  • A week won't appear? Refresh on Cover Jobs names what's off (no tab with that date, a title with another week, no jobs).
  • Admin → Jobs shows when the app last heard from the sheet, and warns after two silent days.
  • Notifications are digests. "Cover jobs" and "Job changes" arrive once the sheet has been quiet for 15 minutes, at most once an hour while edits keep coming. "Cover picks" is off unless turned on in your profile.
  • Bid day moved? Set it on Cover Jobs once dispatch says so. Reminders and "You're up" follow it.

Cover drivers

  • Covers is for reference. Dispatch and the sheet decide. Call dispatch at your call-in time, or sooner once you're told you're up, and ask what's available.
  • Your place and call-in time can move until bid day. The app tells you the final ones once the list settles.
  • "Taken" means a name is on that job in the sheet. A job can reopen. Your list isn't a pick: it's your own order to read off on the call.
  • You see only the jobs you can bid on. Shifter and sleeper jobs aren't shown.
  • Not on the list and should be? Tap "This is a mistake". Something else wrong? Tap "Report a problem". Both reach the admins, who check with dispatch.

The Jobs tab (every bid job)

  • App needsThe tab named Jobs, its header row as it is, and each Job Name on one row.
  • App needsWhole rows sorted or pasted.
    Seen: on 11/3 the Driver and Seniority columns of all 298 jobs were out of step for 67 seconds: the wrong driver on every job.
  • HelpsA driver's name spelled as on the pick list lets the app show a driver their own job.
  • App needsSeniority as a whole number. When the app's Jobs page opens to drivers, they'll see each job's times, hours, route and seniority number, never a name. Anything else in that cell shows blank.
  • HelpsDescription in the weekly tabs' style (Mon-Fri: BETPA>LANPA>BETPA) lets each stop open its building.
