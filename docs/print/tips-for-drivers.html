Post a tip in under a minute

  1. Look up the SLIC you just ran: scan the code, or open SLICs and search for it.
  2. Scroll to "Driver tips" and tap a topic to start: Gate / guard, Parking, Dock / door, Hours, Contact, Heads-up or Directions. Or just tap the box.
  3. Type what you wish you'd known the first time. One or two lines is plenty.
  4. Tap Post tip. That's it. It's there for every driver who looks up that SLIC.

Standing at the spot? Pin it.

Tap Add pin, then Use my location, and the tip marks that exact spot: the hidden gate, the right door, the drop lot. Use it only when you're there. A pin can go up with no words at all, and other drivers open it in their maps app.

Keep the tips right

  • Thumbs up a tip that helped, thumbs down one that's wrong. The most helpful rise to the top. Tap again to take your vote back.
  • Tap Reply to answer a tip: "still true as of today", or "gate moved to the south side".
  • On your own tip, the pencil changes it and the trash can deletes it.

Hear back when someone answers

Menu → Profile → turn on Notifications. Your phone tells you when a driver replies to your tip. It says only the SLIC number, never who or what. iPhone: open SLICs from your home screen first.

SLICs reminds you

Come back to the app after a lookup and it may ask "How was it?" Tap Leave a tip while it's fresh.
