Look up a SLIC

  1. Scan the code and tap Sign in or sign up. Continue with Google sets you up, or tap Create one to use an email and password. There's nothing to download.
  2. Tap the search bar, SLIC, code or name, and type the SLIC number dispatch gave you. The letter code, the customer's name or the town work too.
  3. Tap Open map. The address opens in Google Maps, or Apple Maps on an iPhone. Map routes may not be truck-friendly. If unsure, ask dispatch for directions.

On every card

  • Call dials that building's dispatch. UPS buildings have it.
  • Directions opens the route as numbered steps, for the SLICs that have one. Read it one big step at a time, hear it read aloud, or print it.
  • The copy button next to the address, to paste it anywhere.
  • Something wrong with this SLIC? Report a wrong address or phone number and it's fixed for everyone.

Driver tips: read them before you leave

Notes from drivers who've been there: where to park, which door, the gate that's really the entrance, the low bridge on the way in.

  • Tap Tips on the card. A red number means new tips since you last looked.
  • Each tip says how long ago it was posted. A gate code from last year may not work anymore.
  • Thumbs up a tip that helped, thumbs down one that's wrong, or tap Reply: "still true as of today".

Know something? Post it.

Scroll to "Driver tips" and tap a topic to start: Gate / guard, Parking, Dock / door, Hours, Contact, Heads-up or Directions. Keep it short and tap Post tip. Standing at the hidden gate? Add pin, then Use my location, marks the exact spot on the map.
