# SLICs tutorial video scripts

Six short screen recordings for drivers who aren't comfortable with apps. Each one matches a guide on `/help` (the anchor is listed), so a driver can watch the video and then read the same steps, and each video can be texted on its own.

**Keep every video under a minute.** One task per video. Say what you're about to tap *before* you tap it, and pause a beat after each tap so viewers can follow.

## Before you record

- **Phone:** record on a phone, in portrait, not a computer. iPhone: Control Center → Screen Recording. Android: Quick Settings → Screen recorder (turn on "Show touches" if offered).
- **Quiet the phone:** turn on Do Not Disturb so no texts pop up on screen.
- **Bigger text:** raise the phone's text size a step, and pick light mode so the video reads well when it's watched outside.
- **Privacy:**
  - The sign-in video shows an email address. Record it with a spare account, or blur it when you edit.
  - Driver tips show other drivers' first names and photos. For the tips videos, pick a SLIC whose only tips are yours, or blur the names.
- **Pick one SLIC for all the lookup videos:** a UPS center that has a phone number, a directions PDF and at least one tip, so every button shows up. Use the same one throughout.
- **Voice:** talk like you're showing a coworker in the break room. Short sentences. Use the button names exactly as they appear on screen (they're in **bold** below).

## 1. Signing in for the first time

**Guide:** `/help#sign-in` · **Length:** about 45 seconds
**Start on:** the SLICs sign-in page, signed out.

| # | On screen | Say |
|---|-----------|-----|
| 1 | The sign-in page | "This is SLICs. It replaces the binder: type a SLIC and you get the address, directions and a phone number. First, let's sign in." |
| 2 | Point at **Continue with Google** | "If you have Gmail, tap **Continue with Google**…" |
| 3 | Tap it, pick the account | "…pick your account, and you're in. That's all." |
| 4 | Back on the sign-in page, tap **Create one** | "No Gmail? No problem. Under 'or continue with email', tap **Create one**." |
| 5 | Fill in the form (blur the email) | "Type your name, your email, and a password with at least eight letters or numbers. Type the password twice." |
| 6 | Tap **Create Account** | "Tap **Create Account**." |
| 7 | The welcome message, tap **Use SLICs now** | "You'll see a welcome message. Tap **Use SLICs now**, and you're ready to look up SLICs." |
| 8 | The SLICs search page | "Next time, the app usually keeps you signed in." |

## 2. Putting SLICs on your home screen

**Guide:** `/help#install` · **Length:** about 45 seconds
**Record twice if you can:** once on an iPhone, once on an Android. If you only have one phone, record that one and show the other phone's steps as on-screen text.

**iPhone (Safari)**

| # | On screen | Say |
|---|-----------|-----|
| 1 | SLICs open in Safari | "Let's put SLICs on your home screen, so it opens with one tap like any other app." |
| 2 | Tap the **Share** button at the bottom | "Tap the **Share** button. It's the square with the arrow pointing up, at the bottom." |
| 3 | Scroll the share sheet, tap **Add to Home Screen** | "Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**…" |
| 4 | Tap **Add** | "…then **Add**." |
| 5 | Home screen, tap the SLICs icon | "There it is. Tap it to open SLICs. The first time, it'll ask you to sign in again. That's normal." |

**Android (Chrome)**

| # | On screen | Say |
|---|-----------|-----|
| 1 | SLICs search page showing the "Add SLICs to your home screen" notice | "On Android, SLICs offers to install itself. Tap **Install**." |
| 2 | Chrome's install prompt, tap **Install** | "Tap **Install** again to confirm." |
| 3 | Open the menu (three lines, top left), point at **Install app** | "Don't see the message? Open the menu at the top left and tap **Install app**." |
| 4 | Home screen, tap the SLICs icon | "Now SLICs is on your home screen." |

## 3. Finding your way around

**Guide:** `/help#menu` · **Length:** about 30 seconds
**Start on:** the SLICs search page, signed in.

| # | On screen | Say |
|---|-----------|-----|
| 1 | Tap the menu button (three lines, top left) | "Everything in SLICs is in this menu. Tap the three lines at the top left." |
| 2 | Point at **Look up a SLIC** and **All Hubs** | "**Look up a SLIC** is the search. **All Hubs** shows only UPS buildings." |
| 3 | Point at **Profile** and **Help** | "**Profile** is your account. **Help** has step-by-step guides like this one." |
| 4 | Turn on **Dark mode** | "Driving at night? Turn on **Dark mode**. It's easier on your eyes." |
| 5 | Tap the X | "Tap the X to close the menu." |

## 4. Looking up a SLIC and getting there

**Guides:** `/help#search`, `#navigate`, `#call`, `#copy`, `#pdf` · **Length:** about 60 seconds
**Start on:** the SLICs search page, signed in, with your chosen SLIC.

| # | On screen | Say |
|---|-----------|-----|
| 1 | Tap the search bar (**SLIC, code or name**) | "Dispatch hands you a SLIC. Tap the search bar…" |
| 2 | Type the number slowly | "…and type the number. You can also type the letter code, the customer's name or the town." |
| 3 | Tap the match in the list | "Tap the right one." |
| 4 | The card, point at name, SLIC number and address | "Here's the name, the SLIC number and the address." |
| 5 | Tap **Navigate** | "To drive there, tap the big blue **Navigate** button. Your maps app opens with the address. Just tap **Start**." |
| 6 | Back in SLICs, point at **Call** | "Need to call ahead? **Call** dials that building's dispatch. Only UPS buildings have it." |
| 7 | Point at **PDF** | "If there's a **PDF** button, it opens the directions sheet, just like the binder." |
| 8 | Tap the copy button next to the address | "This button copies the address, if you need to paste it somewhere else." |
| 9 | Tap the search bar again, show **Recent** | "Next time, tap the search bar before you type. Your recent SLICs are right at the top." |

## 5. Reading and posting driver tips

**Guides:** `/help#read-tips`, `#post-tip`, `#vote` · **Length:** about 60 seconds
**Start on:** your chosen SLIC's card. Blur other drivers' names.

| # | On screen | Say |
|---|-----------|-----|
| 1 | Point at **Tips** | "Drivers leave tips for each other: where the real entrance is, what roads to avoid, who to call. The number shows how many tips there are. Red means new ones." |
| 2 | Tap **Tips**, page scrolls to Driver tips | "Tap **Tips** to read them." |
| 3 | Tap **Top**, then **Newest** | "**Top** shows the most helpful first. **Newest** shows the latest. Check how old a tip is: an old gate code may not work." |
| 4 | Tap the thumbs up on a tip | "If a tip helped you, give it a thumbs up. That moves the good ones to the top." |
| 5 | Tap a topic, like **Parking** | "To add your own, tap a topic, like **Parking**…" |
| 6 | Type a short tip | "…type what the next driver should know…" |
| 7 | Tap **Post tip** | "…and tap **Post tip**. That's it." |
| 8 | Point at the pencil and trash can on your tip | "Made a mistake? The pencil changes your tip, the trash can deletes it." |

## 6. Reporting a wrong address or phone number

**Guide:** `/help#report` · **Length:** about 30 seconds
**Start on:** your chosen SLIC's card. **Tap Cancel at the end:** don't send a real report while recording.

| # | On screen | Say |
|---|-----------|-----|
| 1 | Scroll to **Something wrong with this SLIC?** | "If the address or phone number is wrong, let the admin know. Under the card, tap **Something wrong with this SLIC?**" |
| 2 | Tap **Wrong phone number** | "Pick what's wrong: phone number, address, closed or moved, or something else." |
| 3 | Type a note | "Add a note if you can, like the new number." |
| 4 | Point at **Send report**, then tap **Cancel** | "Then tap **Send report**. The admin fixes it for every driver. Tips are for other drivers; reports fix the card itself." |

## After recording

- Trim the start and end, and blur any emails, names or photos.
- Upload where drivers already go (a YouTube channel set to Unlisted works well). Text the link with one line: "How to look up a SLIC, in under a minute."
- If the button names change in the app, re-record that video and update `components/help/guides.jsx` at the same time.
